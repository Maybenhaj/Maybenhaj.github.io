National Park - Poster

Design & Execution Highlights

This illustrative piece captures the iconic essence of Yellowstone National Park through a double-exposure design technique. By blending a silhouette of a grizzly bear with the vibrant, geothermal colors of the Grand Prismatic Spring and the rugged mountainous landscape, the design evokes a sense of wild exploration. Created using Adobe Creative Suite, this project emphasizes my ability to merge complex imagery into a cohesive, balanced visual narrative.
