New York City Architectural Postcard Series

This project features a cohesive collection of six vector illustrations showcasing iconic New York City landmarks—the Chrysler Building, the Empire State Building, and the Brooklyn Bridge. The collection is designed as a collectible postcard series, exploring architectural geometry, typography, and dramatic shifts in atmospheric lighting. Each landmark is presented in two distinct color palettes, contrasting the warmth of a golden hour sunset against the vibrant, cool tones of midnight neon. To demonstrate the artwork's real-world application, the final designs are presented in a clean, flat-lay print mockup on a rustic wood background, emphasizing texture, scale, and tangible paper quality.

Tools & Skills Applied:    Adobe Illustrator  •  Typography
