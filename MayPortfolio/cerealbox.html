Portfolio / Project Case Study

Packaging Design – Seasonal Product Extension

Role: Lead Brand Designer  |  Expertise: Concept, Visual Strategy, Print Production

As part of a seasonal product strategy, I designed this limited-edition cereal box to drive shelf appeal during the winter months. By incorporating winter-themed characters, specific seasonal messaging, and a focus on the product's unique "winter marshmallow" differentiator, the design aims to capture consumer attention while reinforcing the brand's identity as a comforting, pantry-essential staple.

Cheerios "Snowy Season" Limited Edition

01/ Cheerios Seasonal Packaging

This project involved the development of a cohesive, thematic packaging design for a limited-edition seasonal product. The goal was to maintain brand recognition while introducing festive, winter-inspired elements. I managed the full lifecycle from flat-die template layout to 3D visualization, ensuring that all technical requirements—such as nutritional panels, barcode placement, and industry-standard marketing copy—were seamlessly integrated into the final 3D production mockup.

02/ Cheerios Holiday Campaign Poster

A cohesive marketing poster designed to promote the "Snowy Season" Cheerios release. My approach focused on building a "visual atmosphere"—using snowy environments and warm cocoa elements to evoke a cozy, festive feeling. This piece highlights my skills in compositing, environmental design, and typography, ensuring that the promotional message is both impactful and harmonious with the existing product packaging design.

The Challenge was to make a cold-themed winter design feel warm and inviting, which I achieved through lighting choices and lifestyle imagery.
