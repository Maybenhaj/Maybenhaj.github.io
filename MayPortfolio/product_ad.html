Portfolio / Project Case Study

Multi-Channel Digital & Print Ad Campaign

Role: Lead Brand Designer  |  Expertise: Concept, Visual Strategy, Print Production

The Challenge

To develop a highly cohesive cross-platform advertising campaign for a product. The objective was to design a unified visual system that seamlessly translates across digital interfaces, print media, and large-scale environmental advertising while maintaining absolute brand integrity.

The Solution

Using a sophisticated dark-mode UI foundation paired with luxury gold accenting, the campaign establishes a high-end, modern identity. The visual assets were systematically adapted to fit diverse formatting constraints—ranging from vertical social media stories and responsive web layouts to high-resolution print flyers, magazine spreads, mall directories, and retail window displays.

Brand Deliverables

01/ Editorial & Print Media

High-Resolution Print Production. Translating the digital UI aesthetics into a tactile editorial format. The layouts preserve the brand’s signature typography and deep color gradients, optimized specifically for accurate CMYK color profiles across magazine spreads and marketing flyers.

02/ Environmental Advertising

Large-Scale Retail Signage. Environmental mockups demonstrating real-world scalability. The designs are adapted for high-impact visibility in public spaces, utilizing structural scale in mall directories and retail window posters to grab attention from a distance while keeping the core messaging clear.

03/ Digital & Mobile Layouts

Mobile-Optimized UI & Web Placements. Engineered with a strict vertical hierarchy for social media stories and web banners. These layouts prioritize instant readability, clean vector iconography, and high-contrast "Install Now" call-to-action buttons designed to maximize user conversion on mobile interfaces.
