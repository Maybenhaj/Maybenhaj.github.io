Bombora Bay Water Park Branding & Tri-Fold Brochure

Design & Execution Highlights

  • Visual Identity & Composition: Developed a refreshing, summer-inspired color palette utilizing tropical teals, sunny yellows, and coral accents. Dynamic wave motifs and playful custom illustrations (such as life rings, water slides, and beach balls) are woven throughout the background to create an immersive, aquatic atmosphere.
  • Typography & Information Hierarchy: Paired a bold, energetic display typeface for the primary branding with clean, highly legible sans-serif fonts for the body copy. The internal panels utilize deliberate column structures, custom bulleting, and contrasting color blocks to separate ride listings, dining options, park rules, and ticketing tiers.
  • Layout & Editorial Balance:
    • Exterior Panels: Designed a striking cover panel featuring a large life-ring focal point and essential park details, alongside structured, easy-to-read directional text and maps for regional visitors.
    • Interior Panels: Configured a sprawling, continuous interior layout that guides the reader naturally from a comprehensive list of attractions to preparation tips ("What to Bring") and ticketing information, ensuring a seamless user experience.

Tools & Skills Applied:    Adobe Illustrator  •  Adobe Photoshop  •  Adobe InDesign  •  Print Production  •  Typography
